PODIUM - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Who we are

PODIUM is a private desktop tool operated by the owner of the "PlayRush Podium" sports video channels. It is used only by that owner to upload and schedule the owner's own videos. PODIUM is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Data we access

When the owner connects a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, PODIUM receives an access token and the basic profile information that TikTok returns for that account (display name). PODIUM also sends to TikTok the video files, captions and privacy settings that the owner chooses to post.

PODIUM does not read or collect data about any other TikTok user.

3. How we use the data

The access token is used only to post the owner's videos to the owner's own account, after the owner has confirmed each post. The profile name is shown on screen so the owner can see which account is connected. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or analytics about other people.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server and are not shared with third parties. Client credentials are kept confidential and are not published.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok, as needed to publish the owner's videos.

6. Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke PODIUM's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings, Security and permissions, Apps and services, and can delete the locally stored token. After revocation PODIUM can no longer access the account.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
